# Code of Conduct

ALEXIS follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/diegoaguilar2209) or use the repository's private reporting ("Security" tab → "Report a vulnerability"). Reports are handled confidentially.
